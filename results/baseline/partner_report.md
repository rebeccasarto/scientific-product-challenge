# Executive PK/PD Report

## Executive Recommendation

**Maintain 100 mg as the standard dose for the overall population.** Efficacy exposure–response is positive (OR = 2.02 per SD increase in AUC, 95% CI [1.44, 2.82]); safety signal is absent (OR = 1.16 per SD increase, 95% CI [0.86, 1.57], overlapping null). However, the PK model failed the predictive check—patient-level median predictions deviated >20% from observed at some timepoints—introducing uncertainty that may underestimate dose adjustment needs.

---

## PK/PD Findings

**Pharmacokinetics:** 1-compartment model selected (median CL = 0.38 L/h, V = 4.63 L; 100% convergence across 240 patients).

**Exposure–Response (Efficacy):** Univariate logistic analysis shows higher AUC associates with increased clinical response probability (OR = 2.02 per SD, 95% CI [1.44, 2.82]; p < 0.001; n = 157 responders of 240).

**Exposure–Response (Safety):** Grade ≥2 adverse events show no significant association with AUC (OR = 1.16, 95% CI [0.86, 1.57]; CI includes null).

---

## Population Insights

**Weight Effect (Clinically Material):** Across the interquartile weight range, clearance increases ~30% (allometric exponent 0.79, 95% CI [0.67, 0.91], p < 10⁻²⁹). Volume also increases ~31% (exponent 0.80, p < 10⁻⁴³).

**Renal Function:** Creatinine clearance shows only 9.3% CL increase across the interquartile range—below the prespecified ≥20% clinical-materiality threshold.

**Genotype:** Estimated 9.9% CL decrease for homozygous mutants—below the clinical-materiality threshold; p = 0.081.

---

## Dosing Recommendation

| Subgroup | Action | Dose | Rationale |
|----------|--------|------|-----------|
| Overall population | Maintain | 100 mg | Positive efficacy exposure–response; no safety signal. *Moderate confidence.* |
| Upper weight quartile | Pending simulation | 100 mg (standard) | ~30% CL increase observed, but dose adjustment requires exposure-matching simulation. Recommend prospective weight-stratified PK study. |
| Renal function range | No adjustment | 100 mg | 9.3% CL change does not meet ≥20% clinical-materiality criterion. |
| Genotype groups | No adjustment | 100 mg | 9.9% CL change does not meet clinical-materiality criterion. |

---

## Clinical Implementation

Administer 100 mg as currently dosed. Flag patients in the upper weight quartile (>86 kg) for potential exposure monitoring if a prospective population PK study confirms >30% CL increase. No renal or genotype-based adjustment needed at this time.

---

## Confidence and Limitations

**Moderate confidence in 100 mg recommendation.** The PK model failed the in-sample predictive check: patient-level aggregate median predictions deviated >20% from observed values at some timepoints, indicating residual bias and potential underestimation of parameter uncertainty. Analysis used two-stage covariate assessment rather than full NLME population PK modeling, a single-dose level precludes dose proportionality assessment, and covariate effects remain exploratory associations. Prospective dose-confirmation and full NLME population PK modeling are required before finalizing any subgroup adjustments.
