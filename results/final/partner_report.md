# Executive PK/PD Report: 100 mg Single Dose Study

## Executive Recommendation

**Maintain the current 100 mg dose for the overall population** (moderate confidence).

Exposure-response analysis supports efficacy with no significant safety signal at current exposure levels. However, a critical diagnostic limitation—patient-level aggregate median predictions not within 20% of observed medians at all timepoints—prevents higher confidence and requires prospective validation.

---

## PK/PD Findings

**Pharmacokinetics:**  
A 1-compartment model converged for all 240 patients (median CL 0.38 L/h, IQR [0.30, 0.45]; median V 4.6 L, IQR [3.8, 5.7]). Weight showed a clinically material allometric effect on both CL (30.2% increase across weight range) and V (30.8% increase), requiring further evaluation.

**Exposure-Response—Efficacy:**  
Higher AUC is associated with higher odds of clinical response (univariate: OR = 2.02 per SD increase in AUC, 95% CI [1.44, 2.82]; log-odds 95% CI [0.004212, 0.01189]). This exploratory association supports efficacy at current exposure levels.

**Exposure-Response—Safety:**  
Higher AUC shows a non-significant association with grade ≥2 adverse events (univariate: OR = 1.16 per SD increase in AUC, 95% CI [0.86, 1.57]; log-odds 95% CI [−0.001765, 0.005141]). No significant exposure-related safety signal was detected.

---

## Population Insights

**Weight (IQR 62–86 kg):**  
Exploratory two-stage analysis indicates an estimated 30.2% increase in clearance across the interquartile weight range, with comparable increases in volume. This effect size warrants investigation but has not been translated into a numeric dose adjustment; insufficient evidence exists to recommend a specific alternative dose without exposure-matching simulation.

**Renal Function (creatinine clearance IQR 75–102 mL/min):**  
An estimated 9.3% increase in clearance across this range does not meet the ≥20% threshold for clinical materiality. No subgroup adjustment is warranted.

**Genotype:**  
An estimated 9.9% decrease in clearance across wild-type to homozygous-mutant comparison does not meet the ≥20% threshold. No subgroup adjustment is warranted.

## Clinical Implementation

Continue 100 mg dosing. Monitor clinical response and adverse events in real-world use. Do not stratify dosing by renal function or genotype at this time based on current evidence. For heavier patients (upper weight quartile), consider whether prospective exposure assessment is warranted before implementing dose adjustments.

---

## Confidence and Limitations

**Critical Diagnostic Issue:**  
Patient-level aggregate median predictions were not within 20% of observed medians at every scheduled timepoint. This predictive-check failure indicates the model does not capture all observed concentration dynamics and limits confidence in quantitative dose recommendations.

**Additional Constraints:**

- Analysis uses a two-stage approximation rather than full nonlinear mixed-effects population PK modeling.
- Only one dose level (100 mg) was studied; no dose-response assessment of dose proportionality is possible.
- Covariate effects are exploratory associations, not population PK covariate conclusions.
- Weight-related exposure variation is documented but requires exposure-matching simulation before numeric dose adjustment can be implemented.

**Validation Required:**

- Prospective dose-confirmation study in the overall population.
- Population PK modeling using full NLME to address predictive-check limitations.
- Exposure-matching simulation for weight-stratified dosing if clinical need arises.
