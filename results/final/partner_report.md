# Executive PK/PD Report

### Executive Recommendation
Maintain the current dose of 100 mg for the overall patient population.

### PK/PD Findings
The selected 1-compartment PK model is supported by 100% convergence of 240 patients. Clearance (CL) estimation is 0.38 L/h and volume (V) estimation is 4.63 L. Critical diagnostics indicated that patient-level aggregate median predictions were not within 20% of observed medians at every scheduled timepoint.

### Population Insights
- For patients in the upper weight quartile, CL increases by approximately 30.2%. Calculating alternative dosing requires exposure-matching simulations, which were not conducted.
- Across the creatinine clearance range, CL shows a 9.3% increase and does not warrant a dosing adjustment.
- No adjustments are necessary based on the mutant allele count, which indicates a 9.9% decrease in CL.

### Dosing Recommendation
1. **Overall Population**: Maintain current dose of 100 mg based on efficacy data (OR=2.02 per SD increase in AUC, 95% CI [1.44, 2.82]) without a significant safety signal (CI [-0.001765, 0.005141] includes zero).
2. **Upper Weight Quartile**: Insufficient evidence for a dosing adjustment due to a lack of simulations.
3. **Creatinine Clearance & Genotype Groups**: No adjustment recommended; no significant clinical materiality was observed.

### Clinical Implementation
Dosing should continue as is for the overall population while monitoring individual patient responses. Further validation through a prospective dose-confirmation study and population PK modeling using a nonlinear mixed-effects approach is suggested.

### Confidence and Limitations
Confidence in recommendations is moderate. This analysis is limited by using a two-stage approximation instead of a full NLME model, and a single dose precludes dose proportionality assessment. Observed covariate effects are exploratory and should be interpreted cautiously given the diagnostic limitations outlined.
